Gift card terms

  • Gift cards do not expire, and there are no fees.
  • A card can be spent on parts only. It cannot be exchanged for cash, or used to buy another gift card.
  • One gift card per order. Any balance left over stays on the card.
  • If you return parts you paid for with a gift card, that amount goes back onto the card.
  • If you bought a card and want your money back, we can refund whatever has not been spent yet. Contact us.
  • Treat the code like cash: anyone who has it can spend it. If it is lost or went to the wrong address, we can issue a new code with the same balance, and the old one stops working.
